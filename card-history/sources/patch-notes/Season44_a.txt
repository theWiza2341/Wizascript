Season 44
## New artifacts
* Parachutism - At the start of every 3 turns, summon a Mettabot.
* Doom - At the start of your 13th turn, kill all monsters.
* Collection - The first time you summon a tribe (besides All tribes) this game, add a Thrashing Machine to your hand.
* Wiggle - At the start of the game, add Moldsmal to your hand and a Moldbygg to your deck.
* Noodles - At the start of the game, add Instant Noodles to your hand.
* Seedlings - Whenever you lose 10+ HP, summon a Blue Flower.
* Adrenaline - After you play a card, if your hand is empty, draw 7 cards. Can only trigger once each game.
