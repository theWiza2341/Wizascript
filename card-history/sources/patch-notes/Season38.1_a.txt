Mid season
## Shops rework
* Avatars, Emotes and Profile skins have been merged together in one shop : Cosmetics.
* The cosmetics shop will feature daily items, new items and sales.
* The only down side is that you can no longer purchase a cosmetic from the entire list.
## Avatars
* DT cost: 500 -> 375 UCP
* Legendary cost: 300 -> 250 UCP
* Epic cost: 200 -> 150 UCP
* Rare cost: 100 -> 75 UCP
## Emotes
* Cost: 50 -> 35 UCP
## Profile Skins
* Cost: 300 -> 200 UCP
