## Balancing (Spells)
* Melt -- COST 4 > 3. {green}
* Cloning -- COST 5 > 4. {green}
* Multi Shot -- COST 2 > 1. EFFECT UPDATE: Deal 1 DMG to a monster. If this isn't Generated, add 2 Multi Shots to your hand. {green}
* Shootout -- EFFECT UPDATE: Deal 1 DMG to all monsters. If it killed a monster, trigger this again. {green}
* Undyne's Spears -- EFFECT UPDATE: Deal 5 DMG randomly split among all enemy monsters. Summon a Spear with base stats equal to DMG not dealt. {green}
* Explosion > Reload -- COST 6 > 1. EFFECT UPDATE: Burn all other spells in your hand. For each spell burned, draw a monster. Give them -1 cost. {yellow}
* Resurrection -- COST 3 > 2. EFFECT UPDATE: Add a copy of a random non-TOKEN dead ally monster to your hand twice. If you have 15 or less HP, give them -1 cost. {yellow}
* Call of the Grave -- COST 4 > 3. EFFECT UPDATE: Summon 1/1 copies of 2 random non-TOKEN Dust monsters costing 3 or less G from your dustpile. {yellow}
* Same Fate > Faded Memory -- COST 7 > 1. EFFECT UPDATE: Set an ally monster's HP to 1. Give it +1 ATK and Dodge (1). {yellow}
* Soul Drain -- EFFECT UPDATE: Deal 3 DMG to a monster. If this isn't Generated by Soul Drain, add a 2-cost Soul Drain to your hand. Delay: Burn it to heal 3 HP to you. {yellow}
* Hyper Goner -- COST 38 > 12. EFFECT UPDATE: If you have more than 10 HP, choose a card in your hand to burn. Kill all monsters. {yellow}
* Last Dream -- COST 4 > 0. EFFECT UPDATE: In hand, this has +1 cost for each different Lost Soul in your dustpile. Trigger the Dust effects of each. {yellow}
* Royal Determination -- COST 1 > 4. EFFECT UPDATE: Burn your Dustpile. For every 7 burned monsters, summon the next Lost Soul with +1/+1, Taunt, and Haste. {yellow}
