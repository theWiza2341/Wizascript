Season 36
## New Cards
* Soope. COST: 0. [green soul] EFFECT: Look at the 2 cards at the top of your opponent's deck and choose one. Send it to the bottom of their deck.
* Cogwheel. COST: 2. ATK: 2. HP: 3. [white soul] EFFECT: Turn end: Shuffle the cheapest card in your hand into your deck and draw a card.
* Bone Box. COST: 3. ATK: 2. HP: 2. [white soul] EFFECT: Magic: Look at 3 random spells costing <= 3 G and choose one. Add it to your hand.
* Dragon Kid. COST: 3. ATK: 2. HP: 2. [green icon]
* Jookington. COST: 3. ATK: 2. HP: 4. [purple icon] EFFECT: Whenever you cast a spell, draw a card.
* Shrine Mascot. COST: 3. ATK: 2. HP: 3. [white soul] EFFECT: Turn end: Earn 1 G.
* Sugar Pot. COST: 3. ATK: 2. HP: 3. [white soul] EFFECT: Magic: Give a monster in your hand +1/+1.
* Action Figures. COST: 4. ATK: 3. HP: 4. [blue soul] EFFECT: Magic: Silence a non DT ally monster and add a silenced copy of it to your hand.
* Cash Register. COST: 4. ATK: 4. HP: 4. [white icon] EFFECT: Magic: Send a Turbo monster in your hand back to the top of your deck and draw a card.
* Certifloat. COST: 4. ATK: 2. HP: 4. [white soul] EFFECT: Taunt. Magic: Look at 3 random Taunt monsters and choose one. Add it to your hand.
* Evil Blueprints. COST: 4. ATK: 3. HP: 4. [blue icon] EFFECT: Magic: Look at 3 random monsters with a tribe costing <= 10 G and choose one. Add it to your hand with all tribes.
* Goner Clam. COST: 4. ATK: 3. HP: 4. [purple soul] EFFECT: Magic: Restore 2 HP to yourself for each active Future effect.
* Telescoope. COST: 4. ATK: 3. HP: 5. [white soul] EFFECT: Magic and Turn start: Add a Soope to your hand. Turn end: Remove all Soopes from your hand.
* Jumping Rabbit. COST: 5. ATK: 4. HP: 4. [blue icon] EFFECT: Haste. Magic: Add a Jumping Rabbit to your hand and Turn end: Remove all Jumping Rabbits generated by this from your hand.
* Malius. COST: 5. ATK: 5. HP: 4. [blue icon] EFFECT: Magic: Choose a monster in your hand. Shuffle it into your deck with +2/+2 and Candy.
