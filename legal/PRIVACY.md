# Wizascript privacy notice

Last updated: 2026-11-14 (Wizascript 1.8.0, server 1.10.0)

Wizascript is a free, fan-made UnderScript plugin for Undercards. It isn't made by or connected to Undercards' developers. This page says what Wizascript keeps about you, why, for how long, and how to see or delete it.

**Most of Wizascript keeps nothing outside your own browser.** Only three things talk to the Wizascript server: **linking your Undercards account**, the **Card Gallery** (posting, voting, post notes) and **Usage Statistics** (opt-in). If you use none of them, nothing about you leaves your device.

## What's kept, and why

| What | What's kept | Why | Basis (GDPR art. 6) | How long |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Linking your account** (Card Gallery, Usage Statistics) | Your Undercards name and id; a random id for each device you link (only a scrambled hash of its secret); which accounts were linked on the same device | To know who posts and votes, and to count accounts that share a device as one voter | Legitimate interest: running the features you choose to use | While linked. Ask me, and it's removed |
| **Card Gallery posts** | Your cards (text and pictures), your account name, the post's status and any rejection reason | To show approved posts to everyone, and to review new ones | Legitimate interest: a public gallery you post to | Approved posts: until you delete them (Browse → Delete). Rejected: pictures 30 days, text kept for review. Ask me to remove them |
| **Votes** | Your vote on each post, and which account cast it | Up/down totals | Legitimate interest | While the post exists, or until you change the vote |
| **Post notes** | Messages between you and the gallery staff about your post | Talking about a post (and a rejection's reason) | Legitimate interest | Messages are deleted 12 months after the note closes |
| **Usage Statistics** (opt-in) | Each of your own PvP games: soul, win or loss, division and Elo before and after, the opponent's soul, your **starting deck and artifacts**, time | Everyone's card, artifact and soul statistics; your own Profile and History extras | **Consent** (you turn it on and confirm) | Decks: until 8 days after the month ends. Opponent's soul: until the month ends. Games: 12 months after your last game, or 30 days after you unlink every device. Or until you press Delete My Data |
| **Rate limits** | A scrambled (hashed) part of your IP address, with a counter | Stopping abuse | Legitimate interest | 2 days |

**Opponents' names are never sent.** Usage Statistics keeps your opponent's name **only on the device that recorded the game**, so your History can show it. It's dropped after the season after the game ends.

**Published statistics are totals only.** No one's deck can be seen. Today's ranked numbers leave out any card or artifact fewer than 3 players used, so a single player's deck can't be picked out.

## Who else handles it

- **Cloudflare** hosts the Wizascript server and its database (in the US and Europe). Cloudflare is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.
- **GitHub** hosts the Card Gallery's public files: approved posts, with their author's Undercards name. GitHub keeps the history of those files: **a deleted post leaves the gallery, but an older copy can remain in the branch's history.** Ask me if you need it gone from the history too.
- **Discord**: linking reads Undercards' public void (chat room 0) log, as relayed to Discord, to find the one-time line you send. Nothing else from it is kept.

Nothing is sold, and nothing is used for ads.

## On your own device

Wizascript keeps your settings, notes, tier lists, wishlist and similar data in your browser (Tampermonkey storage). This includes the opponents' names above. That data stays on your device unless you export a backup. Uninstalling Wizascript, or clearing its storage, removes it.

## Your rights

You can **see** your data, **get a copy**, have it **corrected** or **deleted**, **object** to it being used, and **withdraw consent** (turn Usage Statistics off) at any time:

- **Download My Data** (Usage Statistics tab) saves everything the server keeps about your linked account, plus this device's part.
- **Delete My Data** (Usage Statistics tab) deletes every game you've sent.
- **Browse → Delete** takes back your own posts.
- For anything else (votes, notes, your account link, the gallery's history), contact me (above). I'll answer within a month.

If you're in the EU and unhappy with how I handle it, you can complain to your data protection authority (in France, the [CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr/en/complaints)).

## Children

If you're **under 15** (France; the age is 13 to 16 elsewhere in the EU), ask a parent before turning on Usage Statistics or posting to the Card Gallery.
