Updates (Buff / Nerf / Balanced or reworked)
Season 15
Beta 15.0 (2017/10/01)
## Classic
* Slower matchmaking but fairer matches (still not based on LV).
* Loyalty points are now available in this game mode.
## Rankeds
* Players in COPPER division can no longer lose DTP.
* Players in IRON III can no longer rank down to COPPER I.
* High rank players should get more DTP after beating a lower opponent.
