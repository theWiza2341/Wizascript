## Balancing (Spells)
* Shopping -- Cost 3 > 0. Effect Update: Spend all your gold. Summon a random monster from your deck with a cost equals to the amount of gold spent. {yellow}
* Feast -- Cost 3 > 4. {red}
* Pie -- Cost 2 > 3. {red}
* Explosion -- Effect Update: Deal 4 damage to a monster. If this kills the monster, deal 3 damage to adjacent monsters. {red}
