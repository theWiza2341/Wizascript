Season 73
New artifacts
* Dealmaker: Whenever a monster costing more than 0 dies, gain a counter. If this reaches 3, spend them to earn 1 G.
* Puppet Scarf: Whenever you or your monsters get more than 1 HP healed to them, deal DMG to a random enemy monster equal to half of healed HP (rounded up).
* Thorn Ring: Whenever you cast a spell, gain counters equal to its cost. If this reaches 9+ counters, spend 9 to add a 4/4 Ice Crystal to your hand.
* Elite Soldiers: Turn end: If you have 3 or less cards in your deck, add 5 random non-DT monsters to your deck and 2 to your hand with +3/+3. Then disable this.
* Seam's Soap: Turn end: If you have 3 or less cards in your deck, give all monsters in your deck and hand +2/+2. Then disable this.
* Glamburger: Earn 1 G and draw a card at the start of the game.
