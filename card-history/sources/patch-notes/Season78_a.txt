* [icon] Toy Knife: Turn end: Add a Change of Winds to the top of your deck if you don't have one in hand.

* [icon] Worn Dagger: Whenever a monster dies (except Lost Souls), gain a counter. Turn end: Spend 7 counters to summon the next Lost Soul.
