## New Artifacts
* Petal Feather (LEGENDARY) -- The third monster you play each turn gains Haste and +1/+1.
## New mechanic
* Quest: This monster will start a quest at the start of the game.
## Ranked matchmaking
* Master+ will now have to wait a few seconds before being able to tag a lower master tier or a diamond player for masters.
* Non master matchmaking is now faster.
## QOL update
* Added a volume slider for music/sounds/jingles.
* Music, sounds, and jingles should now work correctly on mobile.
* New chat design and fixed auto scroll not scrolling when a big message is posted.
* Reworked select card UI in game.
* Effects that require selecting a card in your hands no longer open the card UI selection.
* You can know if you are starting first or second during the mulligan.
* You can now click on in game info icons to get more details (clicking on G will also display the amount of gold you will get next turn).
