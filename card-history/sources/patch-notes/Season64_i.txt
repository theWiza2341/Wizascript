## Keywords
* Loop -- When you play this card while it has 1 or more Loop, add a copy of this to your hand with -1 Loop.
* Program (x) -- If you have (x) more G than the card's cost, spend it when played to trigger this effect.
* Shock / Support -- Now have icons on their cards to indicate their presence. If a card loses its Shock/Support the icon will disappear.
