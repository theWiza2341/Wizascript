* Resurrection -- EFFECT UPDATE: Send 2 random non-generated monsters from your dustpile to your hand. If your have 15 or less HP also add random Lost Soul. {green}

* Another Chance -- Cost 4 > 6. EFFECT UPDATE: Kill all monsters and replace them with random Lost Souls. {yellow}
